# Game ideas

Every idea here is one seat in the same running world. The economy is shared;
each game is a different chair to sit in. Add new ideas at the bottom.

Format: **pitch**, who you play, what the engine still needs.

---

## Business tycoon with pops (the original)
- **Pitch:** Run companies. Watch a living population get hired, get rich, go
  broke and leave you for rivals because of what you did. Victoria 2 pops in a
  tycoon game.
- **You play:** a founder running one or more companies.
- **Engine needs:** a player-run company. Inventions/technology so products change.

## Banker at the desk
- **Pitch:** Papers, Please, but for loans. People and companies ask for money;
  you approve or deny, then watch who pays back, who goes bust, and who you
  turned away comes back as a rival.
- **You play:** a bank, deciding the interesting cases and setting rules for the rest.
- **Engine needs:** loans and a bank. Not built yet.

## Single shop
- **Pitch:** Run one shop. Set prices and pay; everyone else is on autopilot.
- **You play:** one company.
- **Engine needs:** nothing new.
- **Verdict:** not fun on its own, but it's the simplest starting point and a
  building block for the others.

## Mayor
- **Pitch:** Run the town, not a business. Set taxes, minimum wage, print
  money; watch the town react. Frontier or medieval flavour.
- **You play:** the town government.
- **Engine needs:** almost nothing. The viewer's sliders already are mayor powers.
  Taxes would be new.

## Guild leader
- **Pitch:** Decide who may open a business and what they may charge. Protect
  your members or let rivals in.
- **You play:** the guild that licenses trades.
- **Engine needs:** the existing cap on new businesses, turned into a player power.

## Bourgeois
- **Pitch:** Own shares, live off other people's work, climb society.
- **You play:** one wealthy household.
- **Engine needs:** shares that can be bought and sold.

---

## The real problem
Spreadsheet gameplay. Every idea above dies if the player only sees tables. They
live if you see *Hans the baker close his shop because of your tax*. The engine
already knows each person's story; the game has to show it.
